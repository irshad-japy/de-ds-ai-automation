# CV Usage Guidance — Only After You Complete the POCs

## Do not add technologies just because they appear in this roadmap

Before adding a service to your CV, confirm you can answer:

1. What problem did I solve with it?
2. What did I personally configure/code?
3. What failed?
4. How did I monitor it?
5. How did I secure it?
6. What did it cost?
7. What alternative did I consider?
