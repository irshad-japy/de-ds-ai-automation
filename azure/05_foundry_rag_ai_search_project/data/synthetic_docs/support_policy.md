# Customer Support Severity and Response Targets

Priority 1 incidents are complete service outages affecting a production workflow. The first-response target for Priority 1 is 30 minutes during the support coverage window. Priority 2 incidents have a 4-business-hour first-response target. Priority 3 requests have a 1-business-day target. Resolution time depends on diagnosis and is not guaranteed by the first-response target.
