# Shipping Service Levels

Standard domestic orders that are in stock should be dispatched within 2 business days after payment verification. Standard transit is normally 3 to 5 business days after dispatch. Expedited orders are targeted for dispatch within 1 business day. Weekend and public-holiday orders begin processing on the next business day. These are operational targets, not guaranteed carrier delivery times.
